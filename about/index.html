Churong Mao was born in 1995 in Hunan province. She graduated from the Central Academy of Fine Arts and the Yale School of Art. Through the lens of graphic design and digital tools, her works look into everyday life in the physical and digital world. She projects herself into these daily subjects and brings the audience into it, through the shifting of perspectives, she tries to create alternative narratives to reexamine the constructed infrastructure buried under everyday life.
